1. Your Data

SIImole does not run any servers that collect data, and ST-SoftwareTool never sends your personal data, your files or your activity to us. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry.

Everything the software keeps - settings, your app list, logs, the antivirus quarantine and its record of already-checked file fingerprints, and the VPN's Tor data - is stored only on your own device, in the app's data folder.

A few features need the internet to work. They contact the services listed in the next section directly - never SIImole. If you ever have a question or concern about how the software handles your data, feel free to reach out at siimole.official@gmail.com.

2. What ST-SoftwareTool Connects To

  • Update checks: when the app starts, and every 6 hours while it runs, it asks GitHub (api.github.com) - or, as a backup, this website - whether a newer version exists. An update is only downloaded, from GitHub, after you agree.
  • Antivirus: to recognise known malware, ST-AntiVirus checks each scanned file's fingerprint (its SHA-1 hash) against the free Team Cymru Malware Hash Registry. Only the fingerprint is sent - never the file, its name or its contents. The check is a DNS lookup: on Windows through your normal DNS provider, on a Mac (or if that fails) through Cloudflare's encrypted DNS (cloudflare-dns.com). Answers are remembered on your device, so the same file is not checked again. This happens when you run a scan and during the hourly automatic scan, which is on by default and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Your IP address: when ST-SoftwareTool starts, the VPN tab asks ipify (api.ipify.org) for your public IPv4 and IPv6 address so it can show them. While the VPN is connected, it checks your Tor exit address through Tor (ipify or checkip.amazonaws.com).
  • ST-VPN: only while you are connected - see section 3.
  • Video downloads: only when you use the YouTube download command; the built-in yt-dlp tool then connects to that video site.
  • Download Tor (Windows): only if you click it; it fetches the Tor Expert Bundle from torproject.org. The Mac app already includes Tor.

Like any internet request, these show your IP address to the service being contacted, and each service handles requests under its own privacy policy. ST-SoftwareTool does not connect anywhere else: no ads, no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking.

Automatic actions: by default, ST-AntiVirus blocks critical threats it finds - for example by moving the file into a quarantine folder on your device or stopping the program. You can turn this off in Settings. Otherwise the app only changes your system when you ask it to.

3. ST-VPN (Powered by Tor)

ST-VPN routes your connection through three independent Tor relays (Guard, Middle, Exit), masking both your IPv4 and IPv6 address from every site you visit. A built-in stealth mode disguises your traffic using obfs4 or Snowflake, helping you stay connected even behind firewalls or VPN blockers. You can rotate to a new circuit and exit IP at any time with the "New Identity" button, and a live panel displays your real IP alongside your protected Tor IP so you can confirm you're covered before you browse.

ST-VPN requires no account, no VPN company, and no monthly fee - it runs on the free, public Tor network, whose relays are run by volunteers around the world. We keep no logs and have no visibility into your traffic.

When you connect, ST-VPN points your computer's proxy settings at Tor (on a Mac, macOS asks for your password), and disconnecting restores them. Apps that use these settings - such as web browsers - go through Tor; apps that ignore them connect directly. Stealth mode connects through Tor bridges; its Snowflake option also contacts the Tor Project's Snowflake broker (through a content delivery network) and public STUN servers, such as Google's, to set up the connection.

Important: As with any service built on the public Tor network, the final relay in the circuit (the exit node) may, in rare cases, have visibility into unencrypted traffic. This is a well-documented characteristic of how Tor operates, not a limitation specific to ST-VPN. For the strongest protection, we recommend using HTTPS-secured websites while ST-VPN is active.

4. This Website

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver it and protect it from attacks, Cloudflare processes your IP address and standard request information, as any web host does. When you open the main page, your browser asks GitHub's API for the latest version number and which downloads are available, so GitHub also receives your IP address. Downloads come straight from GitHub.

The site uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and has no analytics, ads or trackers. That is also why the privacy notice appears on every visit: your choice is not saved.

5. Your Control Over Your Data

Because everything is stored locally, you're always in full control:

  • View or back up: Access or copy your files anytime from the app data folder on your device.
  • Delete or uninstall: Removing the software and its data clears all traces from your device.
  • Turn features off: The hourly scan and automatic blocking can be switched off in Settings, and the VPN only connects when you choose.
  • No sign-up: No account or personal registration is required to use SIImole.
